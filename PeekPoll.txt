In Java's collection framework, peek and poll are methods primarily associated with the queue interface (and the extended Deque interface).
They are used to interact with the head(first element) of the collection.

peek() => Retrieves, but does not remove, the head of the queue.

poll() => Retrieves and removes the head of the queue.

poll() modifies the collection by removing the element it retrieves.

Error Handling: Both methods are considered "Safe" operations because they return a special value(null)
if the queue is empty, rather than throwing an exception.


Alternative methods
element() is similar to peek() but throws a NoSuchElementException if the queue is empty.
remove() is similar to poll() but throws a NoSuchElementException if the queue is empty.
